Privacy Policy for Sakura AI

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Sakura AI is a browser-based productivity and calendar scheduling tool. This policy explains how the website handles information when you use it.

Information you enter

You may enter a name, email address, task details, meeting details, and guest email addresses. The website uses this information in your browser to provide its features.

Google Calendar access

If you choose to connect your Google account, Sakura AI requests permission to create and manage calendar events. It uses that permission only to create the event, invite the guest email addresses you provide, and create a Google Meet link when guests are included. Google handles authentication; Sakura AI does not receive or store your Google password.

Storage and sharing

Sakura AI does not run its own server or database to collect your information. Some preferences, such as reminder timing, may be stored locally in your browser. When you schedule a meeting, the selected event details and guest email addresses are sent to Google Calendar so Google can create the event and send invitations.

Third-party services

This website uses Google Identity Services and the Google Calendar API. Your use of those services is also subject to Google's privacy policy and terms.

Your choices

You can choose not to connect Google Calendar, remove local website data through your browser settings, and revoke Sakura AI's Google account access at any time through your Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue in the Sakura AI GitHub repository.
